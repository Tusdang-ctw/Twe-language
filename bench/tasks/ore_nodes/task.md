Three ore nodes stand at x = 2, 5 and 8 (z = 0), each with 3 ore. The
player starts at the origin and moves along x with D (+x) and A (-x) at
4 units per second. Pressing E mines the nearest node within 1 unit of
the player, if it has ore left: the player gets 1 ore and the node loses
1. A node that runs out regrows all 3 ore 4 seconds after it ran out.
Count the ore the player has.
