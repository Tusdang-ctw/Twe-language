# `fx.*`, screen shake and hit-stop in 3D; `light.background` (Lanternfall E2 + E6)

**Date:** 2026-11-03
**Line:** Lanternfall engine enablers, E2 and E6 (the last of E1–E6)
**Status:** shipped. The six engine enablers are complete.

## Why

"Juice" is half of what makes the reference battle royale feel good:
damage numbers popping over targets, the camera kicking on explosions, a
frame of hit-stop on a kill. The `fx.*` library (v1.0.1) already did this,
but only in 2D. The 3D shells tick through `host3d::sim_tick` and draw
through `host3d::render_frame`, and neither drained the fx list, decayed
the shake or consumed a hit-stop. In 3D the calls were accepted and did
nothing. A night scene also needs a dark sky, and the 3D clear colour was
a constant.

Reading the code turned up three bugs:
1. **Shake broke replays.** `camera_shake_offset` drew from the script's
   `random.*` generator once per rendered frame. During a shake, the
   game's random numbers depended on the display's frame rate, so a
   replay on another machine could diverge, in 2D too.
2. **Physics ran while paused.** `host3d::sim_tick` stepped it
   unconditionally.
3. **No hit-stop in 3D.** The 3D path never consumed `fx.hit_stop`.

## What ships

- **Effects in 3D.**
  - `ActiveFx.world`: an effect given a `vec3` position (`world_anchor` in
    every `fx_*` builtin) is anchored to that world point.
  - `fx::fx_draw_overlay_3d` draws anchored effects where their point
    projects this frame, skipping points behind the camera, and the rest
    at their HUD point.
  - `host3d::render_frame_dt(…, frame_dt)` runs the visual clocks
    (`camera_tick`, `fx_tick`) and adds the fx layer to the HUD after the
    script's `on render():`.
  - The native and web live loops pass their frame time. `render_frame`
    stays a wrapper that advances one tick (1/60 s), so the 25 tests and
    capture paths that call it stay deterministic.
- **Shake in 3D.**
  - `camera3d::shake_offset` turns the HUD-unit shake into a world offset
    of the eye and target, along the camera's right and up axes, at the
    target's distance.
  - `camera3d::shaken` applies it to the camera the renderer draws and to
    `camera.project` (for drawing). `camera.ray` / `ground_point` use the
    unshaken camera, so aim stays steady.
- **Hit-stop and pause in 3D.** `sim_tick` returns early on a hit-stop
  tick (no physics, no input taken, no script), counted in ticks so
  replays agree. It skips the physics step while paused unless persistent
  states run, matching `frame2d::tick`.
- **Shake determinism.** The noise comes from its own splitmix64 stream
  (`SHAKE_RNG`). `camera_shake_offset()` no longer takes the env.
- **`light.background(color)`** (E6): an sRGB colour, stored linear, used
  as the 3D clear colour and as the colour plain height fog fades toward.
  The default is unchanged.

## Tests

- **`tests/fx3d.rs`:**
  - hit-stop freezes three 3D ticks;
  - physics holds still while paused and falls once resumed;
  - 2D random numbers are identical with and without a shake;
  - a vec3 damage number is drawn at the projected point, a 2-tuple at its
    HUD point, and a point behind the camera not at all;
  - `camera.project` follows the shake while `ground_point` doesn't.
- **`tests/kernel_render.rs::light_background_sets_the_sky` (GPU).**
