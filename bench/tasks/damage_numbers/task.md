Pressing H hits a training dummy at the origin. Hits grow stronger: the
first deals 5 damage, the next 10, then 15, and so on (5 more each
time). Keep the total damage dealt. Every hit shows a damage number: it
appears 2 units above the dummy (at (0, 2, 0)), shows that hit's damage,
rises at 1 unit per second, and is removed 1 second after it appeared.
