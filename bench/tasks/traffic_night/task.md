You are given a working program for this game:

A traffic light cycles green (3 seconds), then yellow (1 second), then
red (2 seconds), then green again, and so on, starting green. Pressing P
(the pedestrian button) while the light is green cuts what is left of
the green to at most 1 more second. Pressing P at any other time does
nothing.

Change it: N switches night mode on and off. In night mode the light flashes:
"yellow" for 0.5 seconds, then "off" for 0.5 seconds, and so on,
starting with "yellow" when N is pressed; P does nothing at night.
Switching night mode off starts the normal cycle again from the
beginning of green (3 seconds of green).
