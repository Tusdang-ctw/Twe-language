Pressing K kills an enemy, worth 1 xp. When xp reaches 3, the player
gains a level, xp goes back to 0, and the game waits for a choice of
upgrade: Z adds 1 to speed, X adds 1 to damage, C adds 10 to health.
While it waits, K does nothing (and neither do Z, X and C when it isn't
waiting). Choosing applies the upgrade and the game goes on. The player
starts at level 1 with speed 5, damage 1 and health 100.
