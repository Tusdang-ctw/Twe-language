Enemies walk a path from (0, 0, 0) to (10, 0, 0), then to (10, 0, 10),
at 2 units per second. Five enemies come, one every 2 seconds, the first
at the start of the game; each has 5 health. An enemy reaching the end of
the path is removed and the player loses 1 of their 10 lives. A tower at
(5, 0, 3) shoots every 0.5 seconds, the first shot 0.5 seconds into the
game: it hits the enemy within 4 units of it that is furthest along the
path (if any), for 1 damage. An enemy at 0 health is removed and counts
as a kill. Draw the enemies and the tower.
