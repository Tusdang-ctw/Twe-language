A boss has 300 health. Each press of Space hits it for 10. Its phase
depends on its health: "calm" above 200, "angry" from 200 down to 101,
"enraged" at 100 or below, and "defeated" at 0 (health never goes below
0). The boss attacks on a timer that depends on the phase: every 2
seconds when calm, every 1 second when angry, every 0.5 seconds when
enraged, and never once defeated. The timer starts at the beginning of
the game and restarts from 0 whenever the phase changes. Count the
attacks.
