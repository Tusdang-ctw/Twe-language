## Notes

- **Sponza:** the Cycles reference is lit only by sky through the courtyard opening, so it is very dark; real-time engines without occlusion light the interior far more. Twe's low error here comes from its dim default lighting, not accuracy (it also textures every surface with one material, a loader defect fixed in M7 session 3). Not a meaningful win until image-based lighting and ambient occlusion land.
