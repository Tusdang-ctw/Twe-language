Beats come every 0.5 seconds, the first at 1 second. A press of Space
within 0.1 seconds of a beat (before or after it) hits that beat; each
beat can be hit only once. Any other press is a miss. Each hit adds 100
times the combo to the score, where the combo is the number of hits in a
row including this one; a miss resets the combo to 0. Count hits and
misses.
