In-game time passes at 1 hour per real second, starting at 6:00 (hour
6); the hour wraps from 24 back to 0. It is night from 20:00 until 6:00.
During the night an enemy appears every 1.5 seconds, the first one the
moment night begins. When day comes, every enemy is removed. Count how many
enemies have appeared in total. Where enemies appear doesn't matter, but
draw them.
