Quests come one at a time, in this order:

1. "slimes": kill 3 slimes.
2. "herbs": gather 2 herbs.
3. "bats": kill 2 bats.

Pressing K kills a slime, B kills a bat, and G gathers a herb. Only what
happens while a quest is current counts toward it (a bat killed during
"slimes" doesn't count for "bats"). Finishing a quest gives 50 gold and
makes the next one current; after the last, the current quest is "done".
Keep a list of the finished quests in the order they were finished.
