You are given a working program for this game:

Five coins lie on the ground at x = 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 (z = 0). The
player starts at the origin and moves along x with D (+x) and A (-x) at 4
units per second. When the player comes within 0.5 units of a coin, the
coin is removed and the score goes up by 1. Draw the coins and the
player.

Change it: A collected coin comes back at the same place 3 seconds after it was
collected, and can be collected again.
