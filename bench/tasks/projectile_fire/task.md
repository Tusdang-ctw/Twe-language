Pressing Space fires a bullet from the origin along +x at 10 units per
second. A bullet is removed once it has travelled 8 units. A target with
3 health stands at (6, 0, 0). A bullet within 0.5 units of the target
hits it: the bullet is removed and the target loses 1 health. When the
target's health reaches 0 the target is removed too. Draw the bullets
and the target.
