Three blades orbit the player at a distance of 2 units, turning at 180
degrees per second (angles measured from +x toward +z, increasing), at
0, 120 and 240 degrees to start. The player starts at the origin and
moves along +x with D at 2 units per second. Enemies with 2 health stand
still at (2, 0, 2), (6, 0, 0) and (10, 0, -1). A blade within 0.6 units
of an enemy deals it 1 damage, but an enemy can be damaged at most once
every 0.5 seconds (by any blade). An enemy at 0 health is removed and
counts as a kill. Draw the player, the blades and the enemies.
