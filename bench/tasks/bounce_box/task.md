A ball moves on the ground inside a box with walls at x = 0, x = 10,
z = 0 and z = 10. It starts at (5, 0, 5) with velocity (6, 0, 4) units
per second. When a step would take it past a wall, it is placed on that
wall instead, and its velocity across that wall reverses and keeps 80% of
its size (the velocity along the wall is unchanged). Count the bounces.
