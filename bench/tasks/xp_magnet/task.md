Five gems lie at x = 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15, z = 2. The player starts at the
origin and moves along +x with D at 3 units per second. A gem within 4
units of the player flies toward the player at 8 units per second. A gem
within 0.5 units of the player is collected: it is removed and xp goes up
by 1. The player starts at level 1. When xp reaches the amount the next
level needs, the level goes up by 1 and xp goes back to 0; reaching
level 2 needs 2 xp, and each level after that needs 2 more than the one
before. Draw the gems and the player.
