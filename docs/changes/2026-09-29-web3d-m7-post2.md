**Milestone:** web3d-M7 ([scope](2026-09-28-web3d-m7-graphics-beyond-threejs.md), [sessions](2026-09-29-web3d-m7-plan.md), previous: [post, part 1](2026-09-29-web3d-m7-post1.md))
**Code:** DoF, motion blur and `.cube` LUTs in `src/kernel/post.rs`; the graph passes, LUT asset loading and display-pass grading in `src/kernel/render.rs`; `AssetKind::Lut` in `src/play3d.rs` and `crates/twe-web`; `postfx.dof` / `motion_blur` / `lut` in `src/stdlib.rs`

## What changed

1. **Depth of field: a physical thin lens.** `postfx.dof(focus, f_stop)`.
   - **The lens.** The focal length comes from the camera's vertical field of view on a full-frame sensor, 24 mm tall. The aperture is focal length / f-number. The circle of confusion is the thin-lens formula, in pixels.
   - **The blur.** A half-resolution scatter-as-gather bokeh (Gustafsson 2018) walks a golden-angle spiral out to the largest blur. Each sample counts where its own circle reaches the pixel, so blurry foreground spreads over sharp background. Background behind a sharp pixel is capped, so it can't bleed onto it.
   - **The composite.** It keeps the sharp frame wherever nothing is out of focus and no foreground spills over. It upsamples the half-resolution bokeh bilaterally (bilinear × depth similarity), so a sharp edge doesn't pick up the blur of what's behind it.
   - **Two edge leaks the test found:**
     - Filtered colour lookups blended a sharp edge into the depth behind it; colour and depth now come from the same texel.
     - A resolved MSAA edge pixel is part foreground while its depth sample can be background; the pass now uses the nearest of the pixel's MSAA depths.
2. **Motion blur: McGuire's reconstruction filter** (McGuire, Hennessy, Bukowski, Osman 2012). `postfx.motion_blur(shutter)`.
   - Per-pixel velocity comes from depth and last frame's view-projection.
   - Tile maxima, then 3×3 neighbourhood maxima, find what can streak into each tile.
   - A 15-tap gather along the neighbourhood's longest velocity weights each sample as a foreground or background cone. So a moving edge streaks over still background, as in a real exposure, where a per-pixel blur only smears inward (the first version did exactly that, and the test caught it).
   - The shutter is the fraction of a frame the shutter is open (0.5 = 180°).
   - Camera motion only.
3. **Colour grading: `.cube` 3D LUTs.** `postfx.lut(path, strength)`.
   - The Adobe / Resolve `.cube` format is parsed (3D, default 0–1 domain; anything else is an error naming the reason) and uploaded as a half-float 3D texture.
   - The display pass grades after the curve, in sRGB-encoded space as grading tools author LUTs, sampled at texel centres.
   - `strength` blends the ungraded and graded images.
   - LUTs load through the asset source like environment maps: `AssetKind::Lut`, natively from the bundle, on the web from the bundle or `fetch`.

The order after the main pass is TAA → depth of field → motion blur → bloom and exposure → the display pass (curve, grading, vignette). Each blur is a new render-graph target, and the passes run only when their setting is on.

## Scores

Unchanged at 0.1850 (Three.js 0.1857). The references use no depth of field, motion blur or grading, and the harness leaves them off. With them off, the frame is identical to session 7's.

## Limits

- **Motion blur is camera-only.** Objects moving on their own need per-object velocity (instance identity across frames), which arrives with GPU-driven rendering (session 11). The same data would give TAA per-object reprojection.
- **Depth of field is round bokeh without highlight shapes** (no aperture blades, no cat's-eye vignetting). The blur is capped at 1.5% of the frame height.
- **One LUT at a time,** no blending between two looks. The `.cube` domain must be 0–1.

## Verification

- `cargo test`: 999 pass (993 before, +6). New tests:
  - `color_lut_grades_the_frame`: an inverting `.cube` LUT turns each display channel v into 255 − v (within 3/255), and strength 0.5 mixes the two halves in linear light;
  - `depth_of_field_blurs_out_of_focus`: at f/0.1 focused 5 m out, a block 25 m away changes on more than 60 pixels while the block in focus changes on fewer than 10 (it was hundreds before the two leak fixes);
  - `motion_blur_follows_the_camera`: a still camera changes no pixel; a camera moving sideways streaks the block's side edges over at least 8 pixels of the centre row, while its top and bottom edges stay sharp;
  - `cube_files_parse`, `circle_of_confusion_follows_the_thin_lens` (a 50 mm f/2 lens against the formula worked by hand), shader validation for DoF and motion blur, and `postfx_dof_motion_blur_and_lut_setters`.
