A platform moves back and forth along x between x = 0 and x = 6 at 2
units per second, starting at x = 0 heading toward +x, and turning
around at each end. The player starts on the platform at x = 0. While
riding, the player moves with the platform. Pressing Space while riding
makes the player step off: it stays where it is. Pressing Space while
not riding steps back on, but only if the platform's centre is within 1
unit of the player (otherwise nothing happens).
